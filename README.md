## 01 — About

I've worked with data and design for more than 20 years, and for over 15 of them Excel has been at the center: databases, pivot tables, sales statistics and cash-flow models built for real businesses. I'm now taking that experience into Python, SQL and Linux, and this profile is where that new chapter lives.

**How I work**

- **Start from the real problem.** Understand the question before choosing the tool.
- **Keep it reproducible.** Sample data, expected results and instructions to run it.
- **Test what matters.** Business rules and calculations are checked, not assumed.
- **Keep it simple.** The smallest tool that solves the problem wins.

### 01 · [Finance Lab](https://github.com/rolandcipher/finance-lab)

<sub>PYTHON · STREAMLIT · PLOTLY · FINANCIAL MODELING</sub>

[![Live demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/LIVE-DEMO-3C8D5A?style=flat-square&logo=streamlit&logoColor=white&labelColor=07121B)](https://finance-lab-frkpyueykw57cnjt25yfyx.streamlit.app/) ![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/VERSION-2.1-5089A9?style=flat-square&labelColor=07121B)

A transparent, editable financial planning and scenario simulator for small businesses. Turn a compact set of assumptions into monthly projections and see how profit, cash and risk respond.

- Monthly income statement, working capital and cash flow, with a direct/indirect reconciliation check
- Pessimistic, base and optimistic scenarios, sensitivity heatmap and tornado chart
- Monte Carlo risk analysis: probability of running out of cash and estimated funding buffer
- Executive report exported to Excel, Markdown and CSV

### 02 · [Data Pipelines](https://github.com/rolandcipher/data-pipelines)

<sub>PYTHON · PANDAS · SQL · ETL · DATA ENGINEERING</sub>

![Tests](https://img.shields.io/badge/TESTS-60%20AUTOMATED-3C8D5A?style=flat-square&labelColor=07121B) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/LICENSE-MIT-324F5D?style=flat-square&labelColor=07121B)

A collection of small, fully reproducible data engineering projects. Each one takes a realistic problem from raw data to analysis-ready tables, with sample input, expected output and automated tests so anyone can clone it, run it and verify the result.

| # | Pipeline | What it solves |
|:--|:---|:---|

| 03 | [Data Quality & Validation](https://github.com/rolandcipher/data-pipelines/tree/main/03-data-quality-pipeline) | Detects duplicates, missing values and invalid records, with traceability |
| 04 | [Analytical Data Warehouse](https://github.com/rolandcipher/data-pipelines/tree/main/04-analytical-data-warehouse) | Turns operational data into a star schema with fact and dimension tables |

### 03 · [MoodZound](https://github.com/rolandcipher/moodzound)

<sub>REACT · VITE · PWA · INDEXEDDB</sub>

[![Open the app](https://img.shields.io/badge/LIVE-APP-3C8D5A?style=flat-square&logo=react&logoColor=white&labelColor=07121B)](https://rolandcipher.github.io/moodzound/) [![Presentation](https://img.shields.io/badge/READ-PRESENTATION-5089A9?style=flat-square&labelColor=07121B)](https://rolandcipher.github.io/moodzound/about/)

A personal Hi-Fi music player that runs entirely in the browser. Your library stays on your device and nothing is uploaded to a server.

- Whole-folder import, reorderable playlists and keyboard shortcuts
- Tag editing and MP3 export with embedded ID3 tags and cover art
- Built-in cover art editor (move, zoom, rotate, crop to 1:1)
- Installable as a PWA on desktop and mobile
